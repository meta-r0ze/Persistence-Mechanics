\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% What did we prove summery (not exhaustive list)
We have derived the minimal architecture of persistence and the mechanism that selects for it. The six pillars are necessary and sufficient; the Entropic Action is the filter; and the Principle of Least Action emerges as the accumulated boundary of what has not yet been forgotten.

% Reveal: It is what sits beneath existing frameworks
Fluctuation theorems, master equations, and Langevin dynamics describe the statistics of transitions; IE describes the structure that must exist before any such statistics can be defined, the discrete foundation that any continuous dynamics must presuppose. This shifts the fundamental question of complex systems from how existing structures optimize, to what minimal architectures are permitted to exist. 

% Reveal: Transfer
IE supplies something complex systems research has lacked: a formal boundary condition for persistence. Because IE maps every persistent system onto the same six-pillar substrate, it functions as a Rosetta stone across disciplines. Hard-won solutions in one domain become transferable theorems in another, the way protocol engineering that
prevents signal decay in a fiber-optic network also prevents fragmentation in a corporate hierarchy. In emerging domains such as AI, where the pillars are present but unnamed, this vocabulary lets systems be engineered for resilience without re-deriving structural knowledge from scratch.

% Reveal: Compare
The same mapping lets persistent systems sharing an environment be ranked, not just described. A firm and a competitor, or two species in the same niche can be compared by how each manages its finite dissipation budget, the way two engines are compared by efficiency rather than by story.

% Reveal: Quantify (failure vocabulary becomes shared and rigorous)
Organizational decline, ecosystem collapse, and the limits of biological longevity are phenomena historically studied through domain-specific narrative and statistical correlation. By defining exactly what is required to persist, IE simultaneously establishes the mechanics of structural failure. Disparate phenomena are recast not as loose analogies, but as quantifiable structural failures under a single, unified mechanics.

% Future work
Natural avenues for the extension include systems composed of nested persistent subsystems and a fuller account of decay itself: this paper treats termination as $\tau \to 0$, without characterizing the trajectory a system follows on the way there, or whether decay has its own structure worth deriving.

% IE provides a missing piece to science
Informational Energetics does not explain why structure arises. It explains why only certain architectures remain to be observed. Persistence requires no intent, no optimization, and no forward-looking agency: only that the substrate be an unforgiving accountant, and that every ledger which fails to balance is erased. What remains is the architecture of persistence.
