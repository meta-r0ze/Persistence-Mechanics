\section{Open Systems: Necessity Without Additivity}
\label{sec:open_systems}
Many persistent systems (cells, firms, ecosystems) are open, exchanging energy and information. But the necessity and sufficiency proofs of \cref{sec:architecture_of_persistence,sec:dynamics_of_persistence} were established strictly within Quiescent Steady State. We show necessity of the six pillars survives unchanged; only the Entropic Balance Sheet (\cref{subsec:entropic_balance_sheet}) requires re-derivation in flux-driven capacity.

\subsection{Pointwise Necessity and Operational Latency}
\label{subsec:necessity_flux_independent}
Because the substrate continuously filters for causal coherence, survival is an instantaneous requirement, not a time-averaged one.

\begin{proposition}[Pointwise Necessity]
For any system persisting past instant $t$, all six pillars must be structurally instantiated at $t$, regardless of external flux $\mathrm{d}_eS(t)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the exponential filter (\cref{eq:survival_prob}), survival over a history $\Gamma$ is strictly multiplicative. If any pillar is functionally absent during an arbitrarily small interval $\mathrm{d}t$, the local survival probability drops to zero ($q_t \to 0$) via the failure modes of \cref{subsec:pillars_necessity}. The entire multiplicative history collapses. The requirement is therefore instantaneous and flux-independent.
\end{proof}

Flux alters a pillar's \emph{operation}, not its \emph{existence}. During phases of rapid growth or unbounded resource influx, a constraint mechanism might be operationally latent ($f_{\mathrm{GOV}} \approx 0$), incurring no active coordination cost. Yet its \emph{structural capacity} must exist at $t$; if the mechanism is fundamentally absent, the system fails to persist the moment it reaches environmental saturation.


\subsection{The Dynamic Budget: A Stochastic Master Equation for Shared Resources}
\label{subsec:dynamic_budget_stochastic}
Strict additivity ($Z_{\mathrm{IE}} = \sum Z_i$) relies on Prigogine's near-equilibrium linear regime, where orthogonal domains prevent interference. In open systems, external flux raises the dissipation ceiling to a dynamic boundary $\mathcal{D}_{\max}(t)$; as operations scale against this moving ceiling, resource contention forces non-linear cross-coupling ($\Delta(t)$) between previously orthogonal pillars:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:open_balance}
    Z_{\mathrm{IE}}(t) = \sum_i Z_i(t) \;+\; \Delta(t)
\end{equation}

We model contention between two of the six pillars, Protocol and Governor, as a stochastic service channel; the remaining four are not coupled in this treatment. Baseline transitions trigger the coordination demands each must process: Protocol routing, Governor constraint checks. By the Palm-Khintchine theorem, the superposition of many independent, sparse demands from distributed subsystem components converges to a Poisson arrival process, $\lambda \propto \mathcal{D}(t)$; the substrate serves these at capacity-limited rate $\mu \propto \mathcal{D}_{\max}(t)$, exponentially distributed as the maximum-entropy prior for a known mean.

We define a shared First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) M/M/1 queue for these two streams; a minimal, analytically tractable model chosen to demonstrate cross-coupling in principle, not to capture the full statistics of real contention, and relaxed below via Kingman's heavy-traffic generalization to non-Markovian arrivals (GI/G/1).


\textbf{The Shared-Stream Coupling:} Protocol and Governor demands, with independent Poisson rates $\lambda_{\mathrm{PRO}}$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{GOV}}$, merge into a single FCFS M/M/1 stream $\lambda = \lambda_{\mathrm{PRO}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{GOV}}$, with utilizations $\rho_{\mathrm{PRO}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{PRO}}/\mu$, $\rho_{\mathrm{GOV}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{GOV}}/\mu$, and total $\rho = \rho_{\mathrm{PRO}} + \rho_{\mathrm{GOV}}$. This continuous-time Markov chain has exact stationary distribution $\pi_n = (1-\rho)\rho^n$ and mean sojourn time $W = 1/(\mu(1-\rho))$.

Applying Little's Law ($L_i = \lambda_i W$) to each demand stream decomposes the shared cost per pillar:
\begin{equation}
    L_{\mathrm{GOV}} = \lambda_{\mathrm{GOV}} W = \frac{\rho_{\mathrm{GOV}}}{1 - (\rho_{\mathrm{PRO}} + \rho_{\mathrm{GOV}})}
\end{equation}
By Landauer's Principle, pending uncoordinated operations incur a continuous entropic cost $\xi_0$; the Governor's coordination cross-term is modeled as $\Delta_{\mathrm{GOV}}(t) = \xi_0 L_{\mathrm{GOV}}$.

This proves the cross-coupling within the shared-queue model: heavy Protocol routing ($\rho_{\mathrm{PRO}} \to 1$) inflates the Governor's entropic cost ($\Delta_{\mathrm{GOV}} \propto 1/(1-\rho_{\mathrm{PRO}})$) even with static Governor demand. Within this model, the pillars cease to be orthogonal because they share a finite volumetric service capacity.

\textbf{Macroscopic Limits and Generalizations:} This model relates IE to three phenomenological regimes, two directly, one by expected extension:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Onsager Recovery (Low $\rho$):} A van Kampen system-size expansion in the limit $\rho \ll 1$ is expected to recover linear force-flux relations ($\Delta \to 0$); QSS additivity is the leading-order behavior of the un-congested lattice.
    \item \textbf{The Thrashing Asymptote (High $\rho$):} As $\mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{D}_{\max}$, the rational divergence dominates: the system expends disproportionate action managing contention, forcing structural pauses (apoptosis, circuit breakers) to drop $\rho$ and avoid causal collapse.
    \item \textbf{Non-Markovian Variance (Kingman's Formula):} Relaxing the memoryless assumption yields a general renewal system (GI/G/1), for which Kingman's heavy-traffic approximation adds the coefficients of variation ($c_a^2, c_s^2$); our derived $\rho/(1-\rho)$ form is exactly its memoryless special case, explicitly isolating baseline geometric friction from domain-specific statistical variance.
\end{enumerate}

The foundational claim of IE that the six pillars are structurally mandatory is entirely unaffected by the specific arrival distributions governing $\Delta(t)$, or by whether this two-pillar model extends to the full six. Within that scope, the Entropic Balance Sheet takes the form $Z_{\mathrm{IE}} = \sum_i Z_i + \Delta(t)$, with the strict
additivity of the Quiescent Steady State as the idealized limit where the substrate's stochastic friction ($\Delta(t) \to 0$) vanishes.
