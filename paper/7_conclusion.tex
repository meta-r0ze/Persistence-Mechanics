\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% What did we prove summery (not exhaustive list)
We have derived the minimal architecture of persistence and the mechanism that selects for it. The six pillars are necessary and sufficient; the Entropic Action is the filter; and the Principle of Least Action emerges as the cumulative boundary condition governing surviving configurations.

% Reveal: It is what sits beneath existing disciplines
Fluctuation theorems, master equations, and Langevin dynamics describe the statistics of transitions; the architecture of persistence describes the structure that must exist before any such statistics can be defined, the discrete foundation that any continuous dynamics must presuppose. For the fields of adaptation and self-organization, this shifts the fundamental question: before a system can adapt or self-organize, it must possess a minimal architecture permitted to exist.

% Reveal: Transfer
This mechanics supplies something complex systems research has lacked: a formal boundary condition for persistence. Because this universal mapping projects every persistent system onto the same six-pillar substrate, it functions as a Rosetta stone across disciplines. Hard-won solutions in one domain become transferable theorems in another, the way protocol engineering that prevents signal decay in a fiber-optic network also prevents fragmentation in a corporate hierarchy. In emerging domains such as AI, where the pillars are present but unnamed, this vocabulary lets systems be engineered for resilience without re-deriving structural knowledge from scratch.

% Reveal: Compare
The same mapping enables direct quantitative comparison among competing systems in a shared environment. By evaluating how competing organisms, firms, or technical architectures manage their finite dissipation budgets, relative operational resilience can be evaluated prior to empirical failure.

% Reveal: Quantify (failure vocabulary becomes shared and rigorous)
Organizational decline, ecosystem collapse, and the limits of biological longevity are phenomena historically studied through domain-specific narrative and statistical correlation. By defining exactly what is required to persist, this mechanics simultaneously establishes the mechanics of structural failure. Disparate phenomena are recast not as loose analogies, but as quantifiable structural failures under a single, unified mechanics.

% Reveal: Predict
The Complexity Trade-off is a pre-observational constraint on structural complexity, coordination speed, and energetic efficiency. A cell, a firm, or a model observed pushing two of these to their limits has already revealed where the third must be lagging, knowing any two constrains the third before measurement begins.

% Future Payoff
The most consequential limiting case is fundamental physics: whether standard physical law is itself a substrate-specific instantiation of this same invariant. Further exploration will determine whether this architectural boundary holds universally: continuously in Information Geometry, discretely in Arithmetic Geometry, and compositionally in Sheaf Theory

% Future work
Natural avenues for the extension include systems composed of nested persistent subsystems, what it takes for a system to arise from disorder, and a fuller account of decay itself: this paper treats termination as $\tau \to 0$, without characterizing the trajectory a system follows on the way there, or whether decay has its own structure worth deriving.

% PM provides a missing piece to science
Persistence Mechanics does not explain why structure arises. It explains why only certain architectures remain to be observed. Persistence requires no intent, no optimization, and no forward-looking agency: requiring only that the substrate continuously filter non-viable configurations. What remains observable is the minimal architecture of persistence.
